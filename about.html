ABOUT MISSION: FOUND

A great business shouldn't be invisible online.

Mission: Found started with a simple, frustrating observation: solid local businesses lose customers every day, not because their work isn't great, but because their online presence doesn't reflect it.

Devon Lambert

Founder, Mission: Found

Army Signal Officer by profession, small business problem solver by habit. I started this after observing my own brother's business get overlooked online for reasons that had nothing to do with the amazing quality of his work.

The Story

Finding the Signal

In the Army, I'm a Signal Officer. Strip away the uniform and the job comes down to one thing: making sure the right information reaches the right place, reliably, every time. When communication breaks down, everything downstream of it suffers. That's the whole job. Keep the signal clean.

Mission: Found exists to close that gap. If your business is better than its online presence lets on, that's exactly the kind of signal loss I built this to fix.

What I Believe

Ownership, not tasks

Google-first, on purpose

I don't chase every platform. Google is where local customers actually search and decide, so that's where I focus most of my effort.

No scope creep

No social media management, no paid ads, no full website builds. I do one thing: local digital presence—and I do it thoroughly.

Real work, not just reports

I don't have to send you a list of things to fix. By way of being a Google Business Profile Manager, I can log in and fix them, then tell you what changed.

Proven before pitched

I'm running this exact process on a real local business before asking anyone else to trust it with theirs.
