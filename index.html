Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is this just about Google, or my whole online presence?

⌄

Google is where most of the work happens. It's where local customers actually search and decide. But we also keep your website's business info, photos, and contact details accurate and consistent with your Google listing, so everything a customer sees tells the same story.

How do you get fresh photos of my business?

⌄

We set up a simple shared photo folder during onboarding. You or your team drop in phone photos whenever you've got a good shot from a job, and we pick the best ones to post. No professional photographer needed, no extra work on your end beyond a quick upload.

I already have a Google Business listing: what happens to it?

⌄

That's the normal case, not a problem. We start with a full audit of your existing listing, then take over managing and improving it. You don't lose your reviews, history, or search ranking. Think of it as us taking the wheel of something that's already there.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?

⌄

No problem. We'll set one up as part of onboarding. Google's own verification process (postcard, phone, or email depending on your business) can take one to two weeks, which is outside our control, but we handle the setup and keep you posted the whole way.
